Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Yarn Studio (“the app,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use the app. It covers data on your device, data sent over the internet (for Premium and stability), and data you choose to send us by email.

We do not sell your personal information.

1. Summary

  • Local-first: Projects, stash, photos, and PDFs are stored on your device. We do not require an account.
  • No ads in the app.
  • No routine background upload of your project content to Yarn Studio servers.
  • Internet is used for Premium subscriptions, subscription verification, and crash/diagnostic reporting (see below).
  • Support email is optional and user-initiated.

2. Information stored on your device

Yarn Studio saves data locally on your phone or tablet, including:

  • Crochet projects, patterns, rows, notes, counters, and settings
  • Yarn and hook stash (including links you add)
  • Photos you attach (from the gallery/photo picker or camera)
  • PDF pattern files you import

This content is not synced to a Yarn Studio cloud account because the app does not offer user accounts.

Camera and photos

The app may request camera permission only when you choose to take a photo. You can also pick images with the system photo picker without granting camera access. Photos are stored in the app’s private storage on your device as part of your project or stash data.

We do not use your photos for advertising or sell them.

Device security and backup

You are responsible for securing your device (for example, using a screen lock). If Android backup or device transfer is enabled on your phone, some app data may be included in backups managed by Google or your device manufacturer, according to their policies—not uploaded to us as cloud sync.

Deleting local data

You can delete content inside the app. Removing all app data or uninstalling Yarn Studio deletes local app data from your device (subject to backup copies your system may retain).

3. When the app uses the internet

Premium subscriptions

To subscribe, restore, or re-verify Premium, the app needs an internet connection:

  • Google Play handles payment and subscription status (Google’s Privacy Policy).
  • Our subscription verification service receives limited purchase data over HTTPS, such as: a purchase token, your app package name (com.yarnstudio.crochet), and platform (Android).

We use this only to confirm Premium access—not to access your projects, photos, or PDFs.

The app may store subscription status locally (for example, whether Premium is active and identifiers needed to restore purchases). If you stay offline for a long period, the app may ask you to connect again to verify Premium.

What we do not do online with your content

We do not upload your pattern text, stash notes, images, or PDFs to our servers as part of normal app use.

4. Crash logs and diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics)

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to receive reports when the app crashes or fails unexpectedly, so we can fix bugs and improve stability and performance.

These reports may include technical information such as:

  • Error type and stack trace
  • App version
  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Android version
  • General device state around the crash
  • Timestamps and Firebase/Google identifiers used to group crash reports (for example, an app installation ID—not your name or email)

Crash reports are not designed to include your project content, photos, or PDFs.

Processing is subject to Google/Firebase privacy terms. In Google Play’s Data safety section, this typically corresponds to crash logs and diagnostics.

We do not use crash data for advertising. We do not link persistent device identifiers (such as IMEI or SIM serial numbers) to your project data—we do not collect those identifiers for that purpose.

5. Optional device information in bug reports

In Report a Bug, you can check “Include device information” before sending. Sending uses your email app to support@yarnstudio.app—we only receive what you send.

If you include device information, the email may append:

What we receive Plain-language meaning
App version Which Yarn Studio version you use (for example, 1.7.0)
Android version Your operating system version (for example, Android 14)
Device Your phone or tablet model (for example, “Google Pixel 8” or “Samsung Galaxy S24”)

We do not receive your contacts, photos, projects, or precise GPS location through this option—only what you type plus the fields above (if enabled).

If you leave it unchecked, only your written description is included.

6. Support, feedback, and deletion requests

Contact Support, Send Feedback, and Report a Bug send email only when you choose to send from your mail app.

Asking us to delete information you sent us

If you previously included device information in a bug report or sent other details by email and you want us to delete or stop using that information where we still hold it, email support@yarnstudio.app with enough detail to find the message (approximate date and subject). We will handle reasonable requests where we control the data.

  • Crash data already sent to Firebase/Google may remain under Google’s retention rules; we cannot erase all historical copies on Google’s systems.
  • Local app data is removed when you clear app storage or uninstall the app.

Accounts

Yarn Studio does not let you create an in-app account. Control of your crochet data is through on-device deletion and uninstall, plus email for anything you sent us voluntarily.

7. Links that open outside the app

You may save web links on stash items (for example, yarn or hook product pages). When you open a link, the app opens it in your browser or another app (standard Android behavior). You leave Yarn Studio; that external website has its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

Manage subscription opens Google Play subscription management in a browser or the Play Store app.

Only open links you trust.

8. Third parties and data sharing

Yarn Studio is intended for users 18 years of age and older, as indicated on Google Play. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 through the app. If you believe someone under 18 has contacted us with personal information, email support@yarnstudio.app.
